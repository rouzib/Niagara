## Scientific dependencies

[Caskade](https://github.com/ConnorStoneAstro/caskade), by Connor Stone and
Alexandre Adam, supplies graph, parameter and context semantics.

[Caustics](https://github.com/Ciela-Institute/caustics) supplies lensing models
and image simulators. The inference example follows its image-fitting tutorial.

PyTorch and optional Triton supply tensor operations, differentiation and
compilation. These packages remain separately installed dependencies and retain
their own licenses.
